# MatchA Visual System

## World

MatchA behaves like a contemporary personal-colour styling desk: garments, dye samples, handwritten editorial marks, and precise archive labels share one working surface. Colour is navigation and evidence, not decoration.

## Composition

- Large editorial product moments alternate with dense, useful archive views.
- Hard rectangular regions and fine rules organize the page; avoid rounded card stacks.
- One dominant garment or look leads a surface while ordinary products remain easy to scan and buy.
- Use asymmetric scale with disciplined alignment and generous quiet space.

## Colour

- Warm off-white and pale dye washes form the ground.
- Matcha green and black carry structure and typography.
- Tomato red is reserved for the primary action, active mark, and important feedback.
- Product and dye colours remain accurate and saturated only where they communicate merchandise.

## Typography

- Heavy condensed editorial display type for short statements.
- Existing body face for readable product and Thai copy.
- Monospace only for dye names, prices, counts, filters, and archive metadata.

## Components and States

- Controls are explicit text, rules, and rectangular fields.
- Selected dyes expand or gain a strong outline and always retain a text label.
- Product tiles have no decorative card chrome; the garment image and factual metadata carry them.
- Hover and focus reveal purchase actions without hiding essential information by default.

## Motion

Use one coordinated colour-change moment: selecting a dye shifts the hero wash and updates the featured garment and grid together. Motion is short, calm, and disabled under reduced-motion preferences.

## Imagery

Use real product imagery from the catalog API. White studio backgrounds may dissolve into a dye wash when contrast remains accurate. Editorial crop and cutout treatments can lead a surface, but never replace real product data.
